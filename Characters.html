About the Characters

Meet the main members of the Soma family and Tohru Honda.

Tohru Honda

An optimistic, sweet-hearted high school student who learns the secret of the Soma curse and brings healing to those around her.

Kyo Soma

Possessed by the spirit of the Cat, Kyo struggles with isolation and anger, but grows through his bond with Tohru.

Yuki Soma

Known as the "Prince" of his school, Yuki is possessed by the spirit of the Rat and seeks independence from Akito.
